## Size-up advice

- **Shirt:** yes, size up one. Kids advance one rank/year but wear this shirt all
  year — extra room helps.
- **Neckerchief / slide:** one size, nothing to do.
- **Cap:** no — it's fitted (S/M vs M/L). Sizing up means it falls off. Measure or
  try on if possible.

## Discounts

- **Annual fall new-scout promo: 20% off uniforms.** Documented for 2025 (ran
  through Oct 31, 2025; new + returning families; online + participating local
  shops). Qualifying items: tops, bottoms, belts, neckerchiefs, slides, caps,
  socks — **patches likely excluded**. Recurs each fall — **confirm the 2026
  dates and whether core-only (vs full) qualifies** at checkout or your shop.
- Alt (2025 terms): 10% off bottoms with a matching top, through Dec 31.
- **Skip the Tiger bundle** ($119.93+): it forces pants/socks/belt/cap and is
  only ~11% off. Ordering à la carte with the fall 20% promo beats it and lets
  you buy just the core pieces.
- With 20% applied, core (shirt+neckerchief+slide) ≈ **$43.18** + council patch
  $4.99 = **~$48/scout**.

## How to batch order

1. Collect each family's **shirt size** (and cap size if they want a cap) into the
   private local tally — `tracking/tiger-uniform-order.csv` (gitignored). Names/
   sizes live there, never in this public repo.
2. Total quantities per item + size.
3. Order options:
   - **Online:** scoutshop.org, one order, ship to one address and hand out.
   - **Local scout shop / council store:** may save shipping; call ahead for stock
     on bulk quantities.
4. There's no standard bulk discount — check whether the **pack** covers/reimburses
   any pieces, or has loaner neckerchiefs/slides, before ordering.
