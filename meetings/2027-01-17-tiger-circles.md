# Meeting 5 — Tiger Circles (2027-01-17)

**Goal:** launch the **Tiger Circles** adventure (Family & Reverence — the Duty
to God adventure). This adventure is **home/family-based**; the den meeting only
introduces it and does the shared act of kindness. Faith specifics stay with
each family.
**Bring:** Tiger adult partners (required).

Requirement text + the religious-emblem alternative: see `adventures/tiger.md` →
Tiger Circles.

> **Why light on den content:** reqs 1–2 are about *your family's own* faith
> traditions and are done at home with a parent/guardian. The den does not
> collect or discuss any family's specific beliefs (also keeps PII/faith info
> out of this public repo). The den's shared piece is the act of kindness.

- [ ] Handout for families: the 3 requirements + the religious-emblem option
- [ ] Materials for a den **act of kindness** (e.g. cards for a senior center,
      cleanup, thank-yous for pack volunteers)

## Sign-off after the meeting

Most of this adventure completes at home. Mark complete once a family confirms
reqs 1–2 done (or the religious emblem earned) and the act of kindness is done.
Record in **Scoutbook** — not this repo.

## Homework for families

- Req **1**: talk about your family's faith traditions; pick 3 holidays/
  celebrations; scout draws a favorite.
- Req **2**: attend a religious service *or* other gathering showing how your
  family expresses reverence.
- (Optional) earn a **religious emblem** of your choosing instead — it satisfies
  the whole adventure.
