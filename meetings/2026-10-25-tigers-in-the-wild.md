# Meeting 2 — Tigers in the Wild (2026-10-25)

**Goal:** complete the **Tigers in the Wild** adventure (Outdoors) — all 5
requirements, in one meeting, **outside** while October weather holds.
**Bring:** Tiger adult partners (required). Dress for outdoors; each scout
brings a water bottle.

Requirement text: see `adventures/tiger.md` → Tigers in the Wild.

> **Field trip:** hold the whole meeting at a park with a short trail, or a
> local nature center (many run free/cheap guided kid walks — ask about a
> 20-min "fall nature" walk; covers reqs 3–5 for you). Fall leaves make the
> tree req easy.

## Prep / supplies

- [ ] **Six Essentials** demo kit: first-aid kit, water bottle, flashlight,
      trail food, sun protection, whistle
- [ ] Printed **Outdoor Code**
- [ ] Ball + frisbee for brain breaks
- [ ] Clipboards/paper + crayons for the animal drawing (req 4) — or send home
- [ ] Trail mix (doubles as the "trail food" essential)
- [ ] Attendance check (private local tracker only — no PII in this repo)

## Agenda (~60 min)

Structured blocks ≤10 min, a run-around game between each. The walk is the
big activity.

| Time | What | Req |
|------|------|-----|
| 0:00 | Gather — **catch / frisbee** as kids arrive | — |
| 0:10 | Opening — sign, quick Oath (2 min) | — |
| 0:12 | **Six Essentials** — pull each from the bag, kids guess its use (8 min) | **1** |
| 0:20 | Brain break — **tag** (5 min) | — |
| 0:25 | **Outdoor Code** — read it, "leave it how we found it" (5 min) | **2** |
| 0:30 | **20-min walk** w/ essentials — call out natural vs manmade; spot animals; pick a **tree** and say how it helps (shade, homes for animals, leaves) | **3**, **4**, **5** |
| 0:50 | Brain break — **duck duck goose** (5 min) | — |
| 0:55 | Snack (trail mix) + **wild vs domesticated** — name animals seen today and pets at home; draw a favorite (5 min) | **4** |
| 1:00 | Closing — den yell | — |

## Sign-off after the meeting

All 5 reqs fit this meeting. Unfinished drawing (req 4) can finish at home.
Record in **Scoutbook** — not this repo.

## Homework for families
