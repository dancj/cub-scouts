# Meeting 1 — Tiger Bobcat (2026-09-20)

**Goal:** first den meeting; knock out Bobcat requirements 1–5. Send #6 home.
**Bring:** Tiger adult partners (required). Kids in 1st grade.

## Prep / supplies

- [ ] Name tags + marker
- [ ] Printed Scout Oath, Scout Law, motto (one per family) — see `adventures/tiger.md`
- [ ] The *Parent's Guide* booklet (youth protection) — one per family for req #6 homework
- [ ] Roster / sign-in sheet (names, adult partner, contact, allergies)
- [ ] Simple snack (allergy-aware)
