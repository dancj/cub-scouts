# Meeting 4 — Tiger Bites (2026-12-13)

**Goal:** complete the **Tiger Bites** adventure (Personal Fitness) — all 4
requirements. Works indoors (gym) in December; req 3 is a built-in run-around.
**Bring:** Tiger adult partners (required).

Requirement text: see `adventures/tiger.md` → Tiger Bites.

> **Field trip option:** a **grocery store kids' tour** (many stores offer
> them free) covers req 1 — find each food group in the aisles. Then do reqs
> 2–4 at the next meeting, or at a nearby gym/park right after.
