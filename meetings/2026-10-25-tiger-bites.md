# Meeting 2 — Tiger Bites (2026-10-25)

**Goal:** complete the **Tiger Bites** adventure (Personal Fitness) — all 4
requirements fit one den meeting.
**Bring:** Tiger adult partners (required).

Requirement text: see `adventures/tiger.md` → Tiger Bites.

## Prep / supplies

- [ ] Food-group sorting cards or magazine cutouts (5 groups) — one set per small group
- [ ] Sink / hand-sanitizer + soap for the handwashing demo
- [ ] Open space or gym for a 30-minute active game
- [ ] Simple snack (allergy-aware) — ties into the food-groups talk
- [ ] Attendance check (private local tracker only — no PII in this repo)

## Agenda (~55 min)

Structured blocks kept to 5–10 min, with free play / movement between (1st-grade
attention spans). Req 3 (be active 30 min) is the big run-around and anchors it.

| Time | What | Req |
|------|------|-----|
| 0:00 | Gather — free play as kids arrive | — |
