# Meeting 6 — Tiger's Roar (2027-02-21)

**Goal:** complete the **Tiger's Roar** adventure (Personal Safety Awareness) —
all 4 requirements. Adult partners attend, so the demos are done partner + scout.
**Bring:** Tiger adult partners (required).

Requirement text: see `adventures/tiger.md` → Tiger's Roar.

> **Field trip:** hold it at a **fire station** (call ahead — most host Cub
> dens). Firefighters show how/when to call 911 (req 3) and what to do if
> lost (req 4). Do the video beforehand at home or at a short pre-meeting.

> **Parent permission for req 1:** the Protect Yourself Rules (Tiger) video
> needs parent/guardian permission. Watch together at the meeting **only with
> permission collected ahead of time** — or send the video home. Get a yes/no
> from each family before this meeting.
